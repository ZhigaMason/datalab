word2vec explorer

Word vectors trained on Wikipedia in English, Czech and Ukrainian (models on Hugging Face). Czech and Ukrainian words are lemmas. Everything runs in your browser.

How to use

Every word is a vector learned from Wikipedia: words used in similar contexts end up close together, and some relations become directions you can add and subtract.

  1. Load a model. Pick a language and press Load. Dim 64 (≈ 14 MB) is a good start; ⚠ marks large downloads. Objective, context and window pick between training variants of the same data.
  2. Vector arithmetic. Type an expression and press Enter to see its nearest words and a map of them. Use + and − with spaces, weights like 0.5*paris, and 0 for the zero vector. cosine compares directions only; L2 compares the stored vectors, whose length tracks how frequent a word is.
